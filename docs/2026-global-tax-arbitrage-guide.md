### Module 2: Architecture of the Tax-Free Triad

The Triad is the definitive framework for 2026 tax planning. To reach 0%, you must align three pillars:

1.  **Jurisdictional Zero:** Countries with no personal income tax (e.g., UAE, Bahamas).
2.  **Territorial Dominance:** Countries that only tax income earned *inside* their borders (e.g., Panama, Malaysia).
3.  **The Nomad Holiday:** Specific 12-24 month tax exemptions for remote workers (e.g., Bali E33G, Croatia).

### Module 3: The 12 Golden Destinations (Preview)

#### 4. Costa Rica — The Wellness Pivot
*   **The Alpha:** Digital Nomad Law (Ley de Nómadas Digitales). 0% tax on foreign-sourced income for 12 months, renewable.
*   **The Catch:** Banking can be slow. You need a local lawyer to navigate the specific decree updates.
*   **2026 Update:** Costa Rica has streamlined the "Ventanilla Única" for nomads, making it the most frictionless entry point in Central America.

#### 5. Croatia — The Mediterranean Escape
*   **The Alpha:** Digital Nomad Residence permit. 0% income tax on remote work for the duration of the permit (1 year).
*   **The Catch:** You cannot renew it immediately; you must wait 6 months before applying again.
*   **2026 Update:** Perfect for high-earners looking for a "European base" without the 50% tax bill typical of the EU.

#### 6. Malaysia — The Asian Efficiency Hub (DE Rantau)
*   **The Alpha:** Territorial tax system combined with the DE Rantau visa. 0% tax on income earned outside Malaysia.
*   **The Catch:** Minimum income requirement ($24k/year) and specific "approved hubs" for the initial landing.
*   **2026 Update:** Kuala Lumpur remains the most cost-effective "Grade A" city for nomads globally.

#### 7. Paraguay — The Low-Barrier Plan B
*   **The Alpha:** Territorial tax system. Low physical presence requirements to maintain residency.
*   **The Catch:** Infrastructure in Asunción is still developing.
*   **2026 Update:** Often used as a "paper residency" to exit a high-tax home country while traveling elsewhere.

#### 8. Mauritius — The Indian Ocean Premium
*   **The Alpha:** Premium Visa (1 year). 0% tax if you don't spend more than 183 days or remit large sums into local banks.
*   **The Catch:** Flights are long and expensive.
*   **2026 Update:** A rising star for European nomads looking for a "no-tax" winter sanctuary.

#### 9. Georgia — The 1% Micro-Business Path
*   **The Alpha:** Not 0%, but 1% tax for "Small Businesses" earning up to $185k/year.
*   **The Catch:** You must register as an individual entrepreneur.
*   **2026 Update:** The most stable legal framework for developers and consultants in the Caucasus.

#### 10. Bermuda — The Atlantic Office
*   **The Alpha:** 0% Personal Income Tax. "Work from Bermuda" certificate is valid for 1 year.
*   **The Catch:** Extremely high cost of living (comparable to NYC/London).
*   **2026 Update:** For the 7-figure nomad, the lifestyle-to-tax ratio here is unbeatable.

#### 11. Cayman Islands — The Global Citizen
*   **The Alpha:** 0% Income, Capital Gains, and Corporate Tax.
*   **The Catch:** High barrier to entry ($100k+ annual income requirement).
*   **2026 Update:** The ultimate status residency for high-net-worth founders.

#### 12. The Bahamas — The BEATS Program
*   **The Alpha:** 0% Income Tax. Bahamas Extended Access Travel Stay (BEATS).
*   **The Catch:** Proximity to the US means higher scrutiny for US citizens.
*   **2026 Update:** Rapidly becoming the "Miami Alternative" for tax-conscious creators.

### Module 4: The 183-Day Trap & Compliance

> [!WARNING]
> **Residency is not a feeling. It is a paper trail.**
> In 2026, tax authorities use AI-driven data matching to track your physical location via flight manifests and credit card data. You cannot simply "say" you live in Dubai.

#### The 183-Day Myth
Most nomads believe that if they stay in a country for less than 183 days, they aren't tax resident. **This is false.** Many countries (like the UK or Spain) use "Center of Vital Interests" tests. If your family, your main bank account, or your "base" is there, you are resident from day one.

#### US Citizens: The FEIE/FBAR/FATCA Reality
*   **FEIE:** You can exclude ~$120k of earned income, but you MUST meet either the Physical Presence Test (330 days abroad) or the Bona Fide Residence Test.
*   **FBAR:** If you have more than $10k in foreign accounts, you must report them. No exceptions.
*   **CFC Rules:** If you run a foreign corporation, the "Global Intangible Low-Taxed Income" (GILTI) rules might still tax you in the US.

#### Non-US Nomads: Exit Taxes
Countries like Canada, Australia, and Germany have "Exit Taxes." You may be taxed on your unrealized capital gains the moment you leave. You must "break ties" formally to stop the tax clock.
